Field notes

The walker starts on HaRakevet in central Modi’in, Israel. The north-up map covers a district centered at 31.9017° N, 35.0062° E. Its geographic extract spans 31.8984–31.9050° N and 35.0022–35.0102° E.

Streets and buildings

Road centerlines, named streets, building footprints and planted areas come from © OpenStreetMap contributors. The locally bundled derived geographic database is available under the Open Database License 1.0. Six buildings have mapped levels. Other heights, balconies, windows, rooftop fixtures, vegetation, parked vehicles and street widths are procedural interpretations.

Ground elevation

The terrain follows a bilinearly sampled Mapzen Terrain Tiles elevation grid. Global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Street-level grades, retaining walls and vertical separation at junctions are approximate.

The walker and all its materials are original procedural geometry. Graphics powered by Three.js (MIT).
